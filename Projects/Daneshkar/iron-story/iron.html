The Iron Giant (1999)

In a quiet small town, an unusual friendship begins when a lonely boy discovers a giant metal visitor hidden in the forest. At first, the machine seems frightening because of its massive size and mysterious origin, but its behavior slowly reveals something gentle and curious. The boy realizes that appearances can be misleading, and that even something built from steel can have a heart.
